Meet the Founder

Nikhil

Founder & Lead Instructor · 5+ Years Experience

Nikhil founded Health Hub with one simple belief — that wellness should feel welcoming, not intimidating. Over the past five years he has guided hundreds of students in Jammu through their first downward dog, their first deep stretch, and their first real moment of calm.

His teaching blends traditional Hatha foundations with modern, breath-led movement, and he's known for giving personal attention to every single person in the room — whether you're 18 or 60, brand new or years in.

Questions? We've got answers.

I've never done yoga before — is this for me? ▼
Absolutely. Our beginner classes are designed specifically for people starting from zero. Nikhil and the team will guide you through every posture — no flexibility or experience required.
What do I need to bring to class? ▼
Just yourself and comfortable clothing. We provide mats, blocks and props at the studio — though you're always welcome to bring your own.
Do you offer online classes? ▼
Yes. We run live online sessions every day, plus a growing on-demand library. All you need is a mat and a bit of floor space — we'll send you the link after you book.
Can I try a class before committing to a plan? ▼
Of course. Your first session is completely free, and we also offer single drop-in classes if you'd rather pay as you go. No pressure, no contracts.
